Privacy Notice

How personal and operational information is handled when people visit, create an account or use Sentinel.

On this pageScopeOur roleInformation collectedHow it is usedSharingRetentionYour rightsContact
Effective: 17 September 2026Version 1.0
This notice is a launch-ready product draft. The operating entity, registered address, privacy contact, hosting locations and jurisdiction-specific disclosures must be completed and reviewed before a public production launch.

1. Scope

This Privacy Notice explains how Sentinel AI Security Suite (“Sentinel”) handles personal information through its public website, account signup, web application, Android application, APIs and support activities. It does not replace a Customer’s own privacy notice to its employees, guards, visitors, clients or other people whose information the Customer records in Sentinel.

2. Our role

For account, billing, website and direct support information, the Sentinel operating entity generally determines why and how that information is processed. For operational information entered by a Customer, the Customer generally determines the purpose and Sentinel processes that data to provide the service. The exact legal roles may depend on applicable law and the Customer’s use.

3. Information we collect

Depending on use, Sentinel may process:

  • Account information: name, work email, phone number, organisation, role, login identifiers and authentication records.
  • Operational information: occurrence entries, incident details, visitor and vehicle records, inspections, access events, alerts, assignments and related notes.
  • Workforce and field information: attendance, shifts, patrol events, checkpoint scans, device custody and location data when the Customer enables those features.
  • Technical information: IP address, device and browser details, request identifiers, security logs, synchronisation state and diagnostic events.
  • Support information: messages, attachments and other information provided when requesting help.

4. How we use information

Information is used to provide and secure Sentinel, authenticate users, enforce organisation and permission boundaries, synchronise authorised workflows, maintain audit trails, respond to support requests, diagnose failures, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Where permitted, aggregated or de-identified information may be used to understand and improve service performance.

Enterprise rollout support, when enabled for an eligible plan, are intended to assist authorised users. They should not make final employment, disciplinary, legal or similarly significant decisions about a person.

5. Legal grounds

The applicable legal ground may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests in providing and securing the service, compliance with legal duties, consent where required, or another ground available under applicable law. Customers are responsible for establishing the lawful basis for the personal information they submit and for giving required notices.

6. When information is shared

Sentinel may share information with contracted hosting, infrastructure, notification, support and professional service providers that need it to perform services under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection obligations. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, to protect people or systems, or in connection with a corporate transaction. Sentinel does not sell Customer Data.

The production provider list and cross-border transfer details will be published once the hosting and notification providers are selected.

7. Security

Sentinel uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect information, including authentication, role-based permissions, explicit company scoping, prepared database access, audit events and protected mobile storage. No system can guarantee absolute security. Customers must manage access, devices and credentials responsibly.

8. Retention and deletion

Account and Customer Data are retained while needed to provide the service, meet contractual commitments, resolve disputes, protect security and comply with law. Operational retention may be controlled by the Customer or specified in an order form. Backup copies may remain for a limited period after deletion before being overwritten under the production backup schedule.

9. Choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, a person may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or review of certain automated processing, and to complain to a privacy regulator. For information entered by a Customer, requests should usually be directed to that Customer first. Sentinel will assist Customers with valid requests as required by law and contract.

10. Children

Sentinel is intended for organisations and authorised workforce users. It is not directed to children for independent use. Customers must apply appropriate safeguards if an operational record lawfully includes information about a minor.

11. Changes

This notice may be updated as the service, providers or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated through the service or another appropriate channel, and the effective date will be updated.

12. Contact

Privacy questions and rights requests should be sent through the support contact in the Customer’s order form or in-product support area. The final operating entity, physical address and dedicated privacy email must be published here before production launch.
