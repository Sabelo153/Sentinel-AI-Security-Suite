About Sentinel

Security work deserves a record people can trust.

Sentinel is being built to connect the everyday details of physical security into a clear, accountable operating picture.

Why we exist

Make operational truth easier to see.

Security teams generate important information every hour: a handover note, an arrival, a missed check, an incident, a response. When those details live in separate books, chats and spreadsheets, managers spend too much time reconstructing what happened.

Sentinel brings those records together around the organisation and site. The aim is straightforward: help teams capture events consistently, respond with context and maintain a useful history without turning routine work into administration.

Clear by default

Simple workflows and readable records for the people doing the work.

Accountable by design

Ownership, timestamps and status are part of the operational trail.

Useful from day one

A real free tier lets small teams begin with their essential records.

Our approach

Build the foundation before the intelligence.

Reliable data, safe access and practical workflows come first. Advisory automation becomes useful only when the underlying record is sound.

  • 1
    Tenant boundaries are explicit

    Each organisation’s data is scoped by company throughout the application.

  • 2
    People stay responsible

    Automation can support decisions, while operational accountability remains with authorised users.

  • 3
    Field conditions matter

    The guard app includes offline queueing because sites do not always have reliable connectivity.

  • 4
    Growth should be gradual

    Teams can start with core records and add workforce, patrol and advanced controls when needed.
